Privacy Policy

Dueable · Last updated 23 October 2026

Dueable does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK in the app. Everything you add stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

What the app handles

  • Notices you create: titles, notes, dates, categories, amounts and reminder settings.
  • Scans and files: photos of documents and labels you scan, and PDFs or images you import, together with the text recognised in them.

How it is processed

  • Text recognition and date detection run on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Images are not uploaded anywhere by Dueable.
  • Suggested titles and categories use Apple Intelligence on supported devices. It runs on your device.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server of ours is involved.

Camera and notifications

Dueable asks for camera access only to scan documents and labels, and for notification permission only to remind you. You can change either in iOS Settings. Without them, you can still add notices manually and import files.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Dueable syncs your notices and attachments between your devices through the private CloudKit database in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access that database. It counts against your iCloud storage and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and export

Dueable does not sell or share your data. If you use the export feature in Settings or share an attachment, the file goes to the app or person you choose.

Deleting your data

Deleting a notice in the app removes it and its attachments from your devices and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. To remove synced data from iCloud, use Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

Dueable is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
